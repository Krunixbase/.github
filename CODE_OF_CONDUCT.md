# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Krunixbase is committed to fostering an open, respectful and professional environment for contributors, researchers and community members.

We welcome participation regardless of experience level, background or area of expertise.

Our goal is to encourage constructive collaboration focused on security, research and open-source development.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Communicate professionally and constructively.
- Focus on technical accuracy and factual information.
- Welcome feedback and different perspectives.
- Help create an inclusive and collaborative environment.
- Report security concerns responsibly.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Offensive or abusive language.
- Trolling or disruptive conduct.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Publication of private information without permission.
- Attempts to intimidate, threaten or exclude participants.

---

## Security-Oriented Culture

As a security-focused organization, we encourage:

- Responsible disclosure.
- Evidence-based discussion.
- Transparent communication.
- Reproducible findings.
- Respectful peer review.

Disagreements are expected in technical communities, but they should always remain professional and focused on improving the ecosystem.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions on participation.
